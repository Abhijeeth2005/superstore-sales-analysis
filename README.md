# Superstore Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate business performance, identify loss-making product categories, and provide data-driven recommendations.

The analysis focuses on sales, profit, profit margins, and the relationship between discounts and profitability.

## Business Objectives

- Identify the highest-performing product categories.
- Evaluate profitability across product sub-categories.
- Investigate why Furniture has a low profit margin.
- Examine the relationship between discount levels and profitability.
- Recommend opportunities to improve business performance.

## Tools Used

**Microsoft Excel**
- PivotTables
- Excel formulas
- Data aggregation
- Charts and dashboard creation
- Business performance analysis

## Dataset

The Sample Superstore dataset contains 9,994 transaction records, including sales, profit, product categories, discounts, order details, and customer segments.

## Key Findings

### 1. Technology is the strongest-performing category

Technology generated approximately $835,414 in sales and $145,455 in profit, with a profit margin of 17.41%.

### 2. Furniture has low profitability

Despite generating approximately $740,148 in sales, Furniture contributed only $18,451 in profit, resulting in a margin of 2.49%.

### 3. Tables and Bookcases generate losses

- Tables: -$17,725
- Bookcases: -$3,473

These sub-categories collectively generated approximately $21,198 in losses.

### 4. Higher Tables discounts are associated with lower profitability

Tables sold without discounts achieved an 18.51% profit margin, while Tables sold at a 40% discount had a -35.49% margin.

This indicates an association between discounting and profitability, but does not establish that discounts alone caused the losses.

## Business Recommendations

1. Review pricing, discount policies, and product costs for Tables and Bookcases.
2. Test lower discount levels for Tables and monitor changes in profitability.
3. Maintain a competitive Technology product range and improve inventory planning.

## Dashboard

## Limitations

This analysis is based on historical transaction data. It identifies profitability patterns but cannot establish causal relationships between discounts and losses. Recommendations should be tested before implementation.
